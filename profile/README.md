# Yongjian Wang | 王永健

Exploring brain-inspired intelligence. Building for the real world.

My research spans spiking neural networks, reinforcement learning, and AI for science, with an interest in how network structure, learning mechanisms, and verifiable reasoning fit together. I also work on computational pathology, physiological time-series modeling, and low-power neuromorphic computing.

## Research & engineering

- **Brain topology for continuous control** — Embedding biological brain topology into spiking networks and optimizing synaptic weights with adaptive random search for continuous-control tasks in MuJoCo.
- **From whole slides to lesion localization** — Working on soft-tissue tumor grading and skin pathology through Virchow2-based multiple-instance learning, conditional segmentation, and DINOv2-based lesion recognition and weak supervision.
- **Physiology in continuous time** — Combining multimodal physiological signals, time-frequency analysis, and neural ODEs to model intraoperative dynamics and investigate early prediction of events such as hypotension.
- **Connecting algorithms and neuromorphic hardware** — Building simulation workflows with LIF / Izhikevich neurons and STDP online learning, with work spanning algorithm design, hardware mapping, and neuromorphic system iteration.
