SNN / REINFORCEMENT LEARNING

让脑拓扑参与连续控制Brain topology for continuous control

将生物脑拓扑嵌入脉冲神经网络，以 ARS 进化策略优化连接权重，在 MuJoCo 连续控制任务中探索结构与学习的协同。Embedding biological brain topology into spiking networks and optimizing synaptic weights with adaptive random search for continuous-control tasks in MuJoCo.

COMPUTATIONAL PATHOLOGY

从整张切片到病灶定位From whole slides to lesion localization

围绕软组织肿瘤分级与皮肤病理图像，研究基于 Virchow2 的多实例学习、条件分割，以及基于 DINOv2 的病灶识别与弱监督分割。Working on soft-tissue tumor grading and skin pathology through Virchow2-based multiple-instance learning, conditional segmentation, and DINOv2-based lesion recognition and weak supervision.

NEURAL ODE / TIME SERIES

连续时间的生理信号建模Physiology in continuous time

结合多模态生理信号、时频分析与 Neural ODE，建模术中生理变化并研究低血压等高危事件的提前预测。Combining multimodal physiological signals, time-frequency analysis, and neural ODEs to model intraoperative dynamics and investigate early prediction of events such as hypotension.

NEUROMORPHIC COMPUTING

连接类脑算法与低功耗硬件Connecting algorithms and neuromorphic hardware

搭建包含 LIF / Izhikevich 神经元和 STDP 在线学习的仿真验证平台，参与神经形态芯片的算法设计、硬件映射与协同迭代。Building simulation workflows with LIF / Izhikevich neurons and STDP online learning, with work spanning algorithm design, hardware mapping, and neuromorphic system iteration.
